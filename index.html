Privacy Policy

RTC Fitness (Rich Trim Coaching) · Last updated: 1 October 2026

1. Who we are

RTC Fitness (Rich Trim Coaching) is a fitness coaching app operated by Richard Trim (“we”, “us”), based in France. For the purposes of the UK GDPR and EU GDPR, we are the data controller for the personal data described below.

Contact: Richardstrim89@gmail.com

2. The short version

  • Your training and nutrition data is stored on your device by default.
  • It is only copied to our cloud if you create an account and sign in. Guest users’ data never leaves their phone.
  • We do not sell your data. We never share your health, training or nutrition data with advertisers.
  • If you connect Health Connect, we read only weight, sleep and steps, and write only the weigh-ins you log — with your permission, which you can withdraw at any time.
  • Meal photos and descriptions are sent to Google’s Gemini AI to identify the food; barcodes and food searches are looked up in the Open Food Facts database. We do not store your photos.
  • You can delete your account and all associated data from inside the app: Settings → Delete Account.

3.2 Account data

If you create an account: your email address and an authentication token, handled by Google Firebase Authentication. If you sign in with Google, we receive your email address and display name from Google. We never see or store your password.

Promo codes. If you redeem a beta promo code we store the code and the email address of the account that redeemed it, so that each code can only be used once and by one person. It is deleted with your account.

3.3 Refer a friend

If you are signed in, the app creates a referral code linked to your account. If a new user enters your code, we store a referral record containing: your code, the two account identifiers (yours and theirs), the date, and whether they have completed their first full week. To decide that, the app checks on their device whether they completed every scheduled workout or confirmed their meals on 7 days in one week; only the result (“qualified”, which week, and whether it was workouts or meals) is stored — none of their training or nutrition details. When the referral qualifies, you receive a Guest Pass ticket. Referral records are deleted with the accounts involved.

3.4 Preferences and app activity

We store your app settings — theme, timer sounds, meal preferences, whether cardio calories top up your daily target, and when the app last asked you for a review — so the app behaves the way you set it.

3.5 Data we do not collect

We do not collect your precise location, contacts, call logs, SMS, browsing history, or device identifiers for tracking purposes (other than for advertising, as described in section 4.6). The app asks for camera access only when you use Snap Meal or Scan barcode, and only to take that photo or read that barcode.

4. Where your data goes

4.1 On your device

All app data is stored in your device’s local storage. If you never sign in, this is the only place it exists and we have no access to it whatsoever.

4.2 Google Firebase (cloud sync)

If you sign in, your app data is synchronised to Google Firebase (Authentication and Cloud Firestore) so it is available across your devices and survives reinstalling. Google acts as our data processor. Firebase data may be stored on servers outside the UK/EEA; Google relies on Standard Contractual Clauses for those transfers. Google’s privacy policy: policies.google.com/privacy

4.3 Health Connect (Android)

If you choose to connect Health Connect in Settings, and only after you grant permission on your phone, RTC Fitness:

  • reads your weight (last 30 days), sleep (last 14 nights) and steps (last 7 days), to show your progress, sleep and daily steps and to adjust your targets;
  • writes the weigh-ins you log in the app, so your other health apps see them.

Data read from Health Connect is stored with your other app data — on your device, and in your cloud account if you are signed in — and is used only to provide these features. It is never sold, never shared with advertisers or used for advertising, and never used to decide eligibility for credit, insurance or employment. You can disconnect at any time in Health Connect’s own settings or in the app, and delete the data by deleting your account.

The use of information received from Health Connect will adhere to the Health Connect Permissions policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

4.4 Google Gemini (AI meal recognition)

When you use Snap Meal or Describe a meal, the photo or the text you typed — including any optional description you add to a photo — is sent to Google’s Gemini API to identify the food and estimate its nutrition. The result is returned to your device. We do not store your meal photos on our servers. Google’s handling of API data is governed by their own terms.

4.5 Open Food Facts (barcodes and food search)

When you scan a barcode, or search for a food that is not in the app’s built-in list, the barcode number or your search words are sent to Open Food Facts (openfoodfacts.org), a free, open food database, to look up the product. No account details or health data are sent. Products you choose are saved to “My foods” in the app. Open Food Facts may see your IP address as part of answering the request.

4.6 Advertising

Free users may be shown ads via Google AdMob. Pro subscribers see no ads. Where required by law we ask for your consent first, using Google’s consent form, and you can refuse without losing access to the app.

  • If you consent to personalised ads: your device’s advertising identifier and related device information are shared with Google and its advertising partners, who use them to select ads. This happens only with your consent.
  • If you decline: you see non-personalised ads. Limited data is still processed for frequency capping, fraud prevention and reporting, but no interest profile is built.

Never shared, either way: your health, training, nutrition, weight, Health Connect or progress data.

You can change your choice at any time in the app (Settings → Ad privacy choices), and on Android via Settings → Google → Ads. AdMob / Google Ads privacy: policies.google.com/technologies/ads

4.7 Google Play (updates and reviews)

The app asks Google Play whether a newer version is available so it can offer you the update. If you choose to rate the app, it opens the app’s Google Play page; any review you leave is handled by Google, not by us.

4.8 Problem reports and feedback

If you choose Report a problem or Send feedback, the app opens your own email app with a short device and app summary and a log of what the app did (screens visited, errors). Keys and email addresses are removed from the log first. Nothing is sent unless you press send in your email app.

4.9 Content delivery

The app loads fonts and icons from third-party CDNs (Google Fonts, Font Awesome). These providers may see your IP address as a normal consequence of serving those files.

5. How long we keep it

  • Local data: until you delete it, reset the app, or uninstall.
  • Cloud data, including referral records: until you delete your account.
  • Deleted accounts: removed from our active database immediately. Residual copies may persist in Google’s encrypted backups for a short period before being overwritten in the normal course of their retention cycle.

6. Your rights

Under UK and EU GDPR you have the right to:

  • Access your data — export it yourself at any time via Settings → Export Data, which produces a complete machine-readable copy.
  • Rectify it — edit any of it directly in the app.
  • Erase it — Settings → Delete Account removes your account, your cloud copy and your local data. You can also email us.
  • Portability — the Export Data file is JSON, a structured, commonly used, machine-readable format.
  • Withdraw consent — disconnect Health Connect, change your ad choices, or delete your account.
  • Object or restrict processing — contact us.

To exercise any right, use the in-app controls or email Richardstrim89@gmail.com. We will respond within one month. You may also complain to your supervisory authority — the CNIL in France, the ICO in the UK, or your national authority in the EU.

7. Health information is guidance, not medical advice

RTC Fitness gives general fitness, nutrition and wellbeing guidance. It is not medical advice, and it is not a substitute for a doctor, registered dietitian or physiotherapist. Plans are generated by the app rather than written for you by a qualified professional. Check with a doctor before starting if you have a medical condition or injury, are pregnant, take regular medication, or have a history of disordered eating. The app shows this on first use and keeps a copy in Settings.

8. Children

RTC Fitness is not intended for anyone under 16. We do not knowingly collect data from children. If you believe a child has provided us data, contact us and we will delete it.

9. Security

Data in transit is encrypted with TLS. Cloud data is protected by Firebase security rules that restrict each person’s data to their own account; referral records can only be changed in the specific ways the Refer a friend feature needs. No system is perfectly secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

10. Changes

We will update this policy as the app changes, and will revise the date at the top. Material changes will be notified in the app.
