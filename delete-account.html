RTC Fitness: delete your account or data

App: RTC Fitness (Rich Trim Coaching) · Developer: Rich Trim Coaching

Delete your account and all its data

In the app (fastest):

  1. Open RTC Fitness and go to Settings.
  2. Open Your data.
  3. Tap Delete My Account and confirm.

Your account and everything stored with it are deleted straight away, from our servers and from the phone.

Without the app: email Richardstrim89@gmail.com with the subject "Delete my account", from the email address you signed in with. We delete the account and its data within 30 days and reply to confirm.

Delete some or all of your data, but keep your account

  • In the app: Settings → Your data → Reset All Data clears your workouts, meals, weigh-ins, sleep and progress, and the cloud copy with it on the next sync. Your sign-in stays.
  • Individual entries (a weigh-in, a meal, a logged workout) can be removed where they are shown in the app.
  • Progress photos are stored only on your phone; delete them in Progress → Body, or uninstall the app.
  • Without the app: email Richardstrim89@gmail.com saying which data you want deleted. We act on it within 30 days.

What is deleted, and what is kept

DeletedYour sign-in account (email, name, user ID); your profile (age, sex, height, weight, body fat, goals); workouts, plans, meals, nutrition, sleep, weigh-ins, badges, streaks and rewards; the cloud backup of all of these.
KeptPurchase records of a Pro subscription, held by Google Play and our subscription processor (RevenueCat), are kept as long as the law requires for tax and accounting (typically up to 6-10 years). They contain no fitness or health data. Deleting your account does not cancel a subscription: cancel it in Google Play → Payments & subscriptions.
BackupsDeleted data leaves our active database immediately; any copy in routine service backups is overwritten within 30 days.
AdsAd data is handled by Google AdMob under Google's own policies; you can reset your advertising ID in your phone's settings.
