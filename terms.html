2. What RTC Fitness is — and what it is not

RTC Fitness generates workout plans, meal plans and nutrition targets from formulas and training principles that are widely used in sports science and coaching — Mifflin-St Jeor for calorie estimates, standard progressive-overload and training-volume guidelines, and adaptive targets that adjust as you log your progress. Free users get full plan generation, the programme and meal libraries, tracking and badges; Pro adds extra tools such as the Workshop split builder, unlimited custom workouts, Meal Plan Flex, unlimited meal swaps and automatic cloud sync.

None of this is medical advice, and the App is not a medical device, a diagnostic tool, or a substitute for a doctor, registered dietitian, or physiotherapist. Plans are generated automatically from the answers you give; they are not written or personally reviewed by a qualified professional. You should read and keep in mind the health and safety information the App shows you on first use and in Settings, and you should talk to a doctor before starting if you have a medical condition, an injury, take regular medication, are pregnant, or have a history of disordered eating. If you feel chest pain, dizziness, fainting, severe shortness of breath, or sharp or worsening pain during any activity, stop immediately and seek medical help.

The App includes a feature that lets you flag areas of current injury or pain, which the App then uses to avoid suggesting exercises that load that area, and to warn you before you add one yourself. This is provided as a safety aid based on general exercise-science matching, not as medical or physiotherapy advice, and it cannot identify every risk for every individual. You remain responsible for deciding what is safe for your own body, in consultation with a qualified professional where appropriate.

Some features (such as describing a meal or photographing a plate or label) use an AI service to estimate food and nutrition. These estimates can be wrong. They are provided within a daily AI API allowance: free users get a set number of AI reads each day, more can be unlocked as described in the App, and we may change or limit that allowance at any time.
